🌱 1. Biographical Background & Early Hardships
Farming Roots in Drought-Prone Marathwada
Born in Tadsonna village, Beed district. Raised in a humble agrarian family, he balanced his primary education at the local Zilla Parishad school with field labor—waking up at 2:00 AM to manage irrigation schedules when power was briefly available and selling farm produce in weekly village markets.
Academic Pursuit & The UPSC Journey
Inspired by his elder brother (the first graduate from their village), he pursued his BA in History and MA in Political Science from Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Marathwada University (BAMU), Aurangabad. Despite multiple initial setbacks across four rigorous exam cycles, he secured All India Rank 20 in UPSC Civil Services Exam 2004.
🏛️ 2. Key Administrative Interventions & Ground Impact
Solapur & Washim: Natural Resource Audits (2008–2010)
Led extensive raids against unauthorized sand mining along riverbeds and dismantled illicit country liquor networks. Prioritized tribal welfare verification and school teacher attendance audits during his tenure as CEO, Zilla Parishad Washim.
Navi Mumbai (NMMC): Civic Finances & Citizen Governance (2016)
Audited property tax evasions, removed commercial encroachments, and initiated the direct "Walk with Commissioner" grievance redressal open house. When 105 corporators passed a No-Confidence Motion, thousands of local residents mobilized peaceful public demonstrations to support his administrative measures.
Pune (PMPML) & Nagpur: Public Infrastructure & COVID Response
Standardized scheduled bus operations in Pune to cut contractor dead mileage. Spearheaded emergency containment infrastructure and medical procurement during early COVID-19 management in Nagpur.
Maharashtra FDA: Food Safety & Pharma Transparency (2026)
Enforced statewide prohibitions on adulterated food substitutes (analogue fake paneer), audited kitchen hygiene protocols across commercial restaurants, and cancelled operational licenses for pharma manufacturers failing strict purity benchmarks.
