Chapter 03

By the Numbers — and Where the Numbers Disagree

"25 transfers" is the figure most 2026 coverage repeats. It isn't the only one on record.

Why the count moves. Outlets differ on whether a "posting" includes short-term additional charges, training assignments, and secretariat deputations, and each new report simply adds to whatever base the last one used. The one figure every version agrees on: across roughly two decades, Mundhe's average tenure in a post has run well under the three-year norm the IAS itself prescribes.

Chapter 07

A Note on Sourcing & Privacy

How this dossier was built, and what it deliberately leaves out

  • Transfer/posting counts vary by source — reported figures range from 15 to 27 across different years and outlets. We show the range rather than picking one number to sound definitive.
  • Family privacy. Mundhe is reported to be married with children. Because coverage of his immediate family is inconsistent — one source explicitly states these details are kept private — this dossier does not publish names or personal details of family members who are not themselves public officials.
  • The 75 Questions are prompts, not a transcript. They were written for this dossier as a research aid for interviewers; they are not quotes, and no answers are attributed to Mundhe anywhere in this piece.
  • Dates on mid-career postings (particularly early district assignments) are reported inconsistently across profiles; where we could not confirm a specific date range, we've said so rather than guessing.
  • Current posting reflects reporting current to mid-August 2026. Given how frequently this officer has been reposted, treat "current" as provisional.
