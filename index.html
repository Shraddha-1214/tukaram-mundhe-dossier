🌱 1. The Early Life: Hardship & Humble Beginnings
The Weekly Market Hustle (Tadsonna Village, Beed)
Born into a farming household in drought-prone Beed. Before walking to his Marathi-medium Zilla Parishad school, he helped water crops during late-night power slots and sold farm vegetables in weekly local markets.
Failing 3 Times Before AIR 20
Studied in Aurangabad (BAMU) while managing strict financial constraints. Failed Prelims in his 1st attempt, failed Mains in his 2nd, reached the interview in his 3rd, and finally secured All India Rank 20 on his 4th attempt.
⚡ 2. The Battles Most People Only Read Headlines About
Solapur & The Liquor/Sand Mafia (2008–2010)
Cracked down on unregistered sand extraction on dry riverbeds and unlicensed liquor breweries. Faced open intimidation and administrative pushback.
Navi Mumbai: 105 Corporators vs. 1 IAS Officer (2016)
Cut illegal commercial water connections and audited property taxes. 105 out of 111 corporators passed a No-Confidence Motion to expel him. Citizens took to the streets to stop his transfer.
FDA Crackdown: What are Indians Eating? (Recent)
Raid drives against adulterated food products, fake paneer (analog cheese), and counterfeit drugs.
🎙️ 3. The Unfiltered Question Arc (In Raj's Direct Style)
Childhood & Hunger

The Personal Cost of Integrity
"People make memes about your 25 transfers. But practically—how do you look at your family and tell them to pack their bags every 9 months because you refused to compromise?"
Goal: Get past the stoic officer persona to the real emotional and family cost.
Where Does Public Money Actually Go?
"Why do Indian roads break every single monsoon? Is it bad engineers, corrupt contractors, or are tenders designed to fail so people can eat repair budgets?"
Goal: Demystify the practical mechanics of civic budgets in plain Hindi.
The Food Adulteration Reality

Goal: High-friction, viral topic that affects every listener directly.
