🌱 1. The Early Life: Hardship & Humble Beginnings
The Weekly Market Hustle (Tadsonna Village, Beed)
Born into a farming household in drought-prone Beed. Before walking barefoot to his Marathi-medium Zilla Parishad school, he helped water crops during 2:00 AM power slots and sold farm vegetables in weekly village markets.
Failing 3 Times Before AIR 20
Studied History and Political Science at BAMU Aurangabad under financial distress. Cleared UPSC 2005 with All India Rank 20 without high-cost coaching networks.
⚡ 2. The Ground Battles Behind the Headlines
Solapur: Riverbed Sand Mafia & Illicit Distilleries (2008–2010)
Raided illegal sand-mining syndicates and illicit liquor networks, facing direct threats and administrative resistance.
Navi Mumbai: 105 Corporators vs. 1 IAS Officer (2016)
Cut illegal commercial water lines and audited property tax evasion. After 105 corporators passed a No-Confidence Motion, thousands of citizens protested on the streets to back him.
Maharashtra FDA: Food & Pharma Crackdown (2026)
Enforced a state-wide ban on fake analogue paneer, conducted surprise audits across kitchen chains, and cancelled licenses of pharma units with contaminated drugs.
